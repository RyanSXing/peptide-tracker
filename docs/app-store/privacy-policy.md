# Privacy Policy Draft

Peptide Tracker collects user-entered account and health-tracking information to provide app functionality, including protocol records, inventory, schedules, reminders, and injection history.

Data collected may include email address, user identifier, protocol names, dose amounts, schedule information, inventory records, injection logs, optional injection-site notes, and app settings. Data is stored with Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore under the user's account. Firebase may process network and diagnostic information required to provide authentication and database services.

Peptide Tracker does not sell personal data, does not use data for third-party advertising, and does not track users across apps or websites.

Users can delete their account and associated app data in Settings > Data > Delete Account. Users can also clear tracking data without deleting their account in Settings > Data > Clear Tracking Data.

Peptide Tracker is a personal logging tool and is not medical advice. Users should consult a licensed clinician before making medical decisions or changing any dose, schedule, or treatment plan.
